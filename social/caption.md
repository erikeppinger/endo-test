## English (international)

“Period pain is normal.” “It’s all in your head.” “Just get pregnant.”

Many people with endometriosis hear lines like these for years before anyone takes a proper look. About 1 in 10 women and girls of reproductive age have it, and a diagnosis often takes many years.

**FightEndo** is a free, open-source app that
✅ records your symptoms based on international guidelines (NICE, ESHRE),
✅ documents doctor visits, refusals and dismissive comments word for word,
✅ turns all of it into a letter to your health insurer or doctor, backed by law, fundamental rights and research.

🔒 Everything stays on your phone: no cloud, no account, no ads, no tracking.
🌍 Germany is just the beginning. Know the health law in your country? Add it, since every country is its own file.

AI should make our lives easier. It should not be used to undress people or misuse their identities. We will keep pushing the system until it acknowledges the problem: factual, evidence-based, in writing, again and again.
